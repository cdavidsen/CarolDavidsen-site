Cost of Search Carol Davidsen

About

Carol Davidsen builds the data infrastructure large organizations run on, then writes about what building it teaches about search, trust, and cost. As Director of Data Integration and Media Targeting for the 2012 Obama campaign, she built the platforms that matched set-top-box viewership data to television rate cards to find underpriced advertising slots. She later led audience measurement and data products at Navic Networks and comScore/Rentrak, and founded and ran the data startup Cir.cl. For the past seven years she has built enterprise customer data and CRM platforms inside Fortune 100 global financial institutions, consolidating dozens of legacy systems into unified, governed customer datasets. Through Data Advisory LLC, she also advises mission-driven organizations and nonprofits on data strategy and AI enablement. Current and past clients are listed on LinkedIn.

Cost of Search Carol Davidsen

About

Carol Davidsen builds the data infrastructure large organizations run on, then writes about what building it teaches about search, trust, and cost. As Director of Data Integration and Media Targeting for the 2012 Obama campaign, she built the platforms that matched set-top-box viewership data to television rate cards to find underpriced advertising slots. She later led audience measurement and data products at Navic Networks and comScore/Rentrak, founded and ran the data startup Cir.cl, and spent the past seven years building enterprise customer data and CRM platforms inside BNY and Marsh McLennan, including Marsh's $25 million consolidation of more than thirty legacy systems across four global businesses. She now advises executives on data strategy and AI enablement through Data Advisory LLC.

Writing

Essays on data, AI strategy, and the economics underneath both — written from two decades spent building the data infrastructure behind political campaigns, television advertising, and enterprise customer platforms.
